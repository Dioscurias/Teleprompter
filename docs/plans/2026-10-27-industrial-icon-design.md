# Industrial Teleprompter Icon

The icon should feel like operational broadcast hardware: flat, restrained, and legible at menu-bar scale. A near-black field carries a geometric prompter screen in off-white, three script lines, a compact support, and one small orange tally indicator.

The design intentionally avoids gradients, glow, glass, film perforations, play symbols, text, and photorealistic rendering. Geometry and contrast carry the identity.
